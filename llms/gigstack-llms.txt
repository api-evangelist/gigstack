- Descarga Masiva SAT [Deactivate Descarga Masiva SAT](https://docs.gigstack.io/deactivate-descarga-masiva-sat-32396699e0.md): Deactivate Descarga Masiva SAT for your team. - Descarga Masiva SAT [Get schedule configuration](https://docs.gigstack.io/get-schedule-configuration-32396702e0.md): Returns the current scheduled download configuration plus FIEL and registration status. Use this to check setup progress before configuring a schedule or submitting download requests.
- Descarga Masiva SAT [Save schedule configuration](https://docs.gigstack.io/save-schedule-configuration-32396703e0.md): Create or update the daily scheduled download. The schedule runs once per day at the specified time (America/Mexico_City timezone) and downloads invoices from the last `days_back` days.
- Descarga Masiva SAT [Get download request history](https://docs.gigstack.io/get-download-request-history-32396704e0.md): Returns the last 20 download requests for your team, ordered by most recent first.
- Descarga Masiva SAT [Create bulk download request](https://docs.gigstack.io/create-bulk-download-request-32396705e0.md): Submit a bulk download request to the SAT. The SAT processes these asynchronously — use `GET /invoices/download/schedule/history` to poll for status updates.

- Descarga Masiva SAT [Get single invoice from SAT](https://docs.gigstack.io/get-single-invoice-from-sat-32396708e0.md): Fetch a single invoice's XML from the SAT by UUID. Useful for retrieving a specific CFDI without submitting a full bulk download request.
- Descarga Masiva SAT [Debug registration status](https://docs.gigstack.io/debug-registration-status-32396709e0.md): Returns detailed debug information about your team's Descarga Masiva setup: FIEL status, registration status, schedule config, and SAT connectivity. Intended for troubleshooting.
- Descarga Masiva SAT [Enable SAT sync](https://docs.gigstack.io/enable-sat-sync-32396710e0.md): Enables automatic SAT synchronization for your team. This is a lower-level toggle — in most flows the schedule configuration (`PUT /invoices/download/schedule`) is the right endpoint to use.

- SAT Lists [List SAT lists and sync status](https://docs.gigstack.io/list-sat-lists-and-sync-status-39959228e0.md): Returns every SAT list definition tracked by gigstack, together with the metadata from its most recent sync.
